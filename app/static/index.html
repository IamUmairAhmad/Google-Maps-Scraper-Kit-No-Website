Google Maps business search

Open this page in Google Chrome. Enter a niche and a location. Chrome searches Google Maps and reads every listing. Click Checked, No website, Has website or Closed to see that group and download just those results.

One search at a time. A large run can temporarily rate-limit this machine’s IP on Google Maps. Phones in the CSV are personal data — use them only in line with the rules where you contact people. Maps searches usually stop around 120 listings.

A Chrome window opens and reads Google Maps. Closed means permanently or temporarily closed. A Facebook, Instagram, or other profile link does not count as a website. Email stays blank because no website is opened. Each name links to its Maps listing.
