Google Maps business search

Enter a niche and a location. A small Chrome window opens Google Maps and reads every listing. Click Checked, No website, Has website or Closed to see that group and download just those results.

Keep this tab open while a search runs, and leave the small Maps window alone (you can keep working in other windows). One search at a time. A large run can temporarily rate-limit your IP on Google Maps. Phones in the files are personal data — use them only in line with the rules where you contact people. Maps searches usually stop around 120 listings.

Closed means permanently or temporarily closed. A Facebook, Instagram, or other profile link does not count as a website. Email stays blank because no website is opened. In the Excel file each name links to its Maps listing; CSV files keep the link in the maps_url column.
